How to read these numbers

  • R is the risk on one trade: the distance from entry to stop loss. A take profit is +2R, a stop loss is −1R.
  • The % view multiplies R by a fixed risk per trade. At 0.5% risk, +2R is +1% of the account. Risk stays fixed on every trade: results are not compounded.
  • Trades closed any other way (7-day time stop, manual close) are shown at their actual R.
  • Click a month or a coin to filter the whole page. All times are UTC.
