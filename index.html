How to read these numbers

  • R is the risk planned on each trade: the distance from entry to stop loss. A take profit is +2R, a stop loss is −1R.
  • Trades closed any other way (7-day time stop, manual close) are shown at their actual R.
  • Results are gross: exchange fees and slippage are not deducted, because they differ per account.
  • Every signal sent to members is counted, including cancelled ones. Open signals appear here once they close.
  • All times are UTC.
